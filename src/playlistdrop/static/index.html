Getting tracks onto your iPhone

  1. Tap the orange size sticker on a track to save it, or Save all for the whole playlist as one ZIP.
  2. Safari asks to download. Files land in Files › Downloads.
  3. Tap a ZIP once in Files and it opens into a folder.
  4. Videos into Photos: open the video in Files, then Share › Save Video.
  5. Music plays straight from Files. VLC or Documents can play the folder as a playlist.

Open this page from your Home Screen: in Safari tap Share › Add to Home Screen.
