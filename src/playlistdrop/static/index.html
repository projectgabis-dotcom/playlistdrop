Getting files onto your iPhone

  1. Tap Save on a finished item, or Save all for one ZIP of the whole playlist.
  2. Safari asks to download it. Files land in Files › Downloads.
  3. For a ZIP, tap it once in Files and it unpacks into a folder.
  4. Videos to Photos: open the video in Files, tap Share › Save Video.
  5. Music: audio files play straight from Files; VLC or Documents can build playlists from the folder.

Tip: in Safari tap Share › Add to Home Screen to open PlaylistDrop like an app.
